ACC Tiebreaker Rules

How ACC's published tiebreaker procedure works, and exactly how SportsSimulate's 2025 season simulation evaluates every step - written as reference material independent of any particular week's scenarios.

  1. 1

    Head-to-head record

    If the tied teams played each other this season, that result decides it directly - among three or more tied teams, this is each team's combined record against just the others in the tie.

  2. 2

    Combined record against common conference opponents

    Each tied team's record against every conference opponent that all of them played is compared, treating those shared opponents as a single group.

  3. 3

    Record against the next-highest-placed common opponent, working down the standings

    Starting from the best-placed team the tied group has in common, each tied team's record against that opponent is compared - if that's still even, the process repeats against the next-best common opponent, and so on down the standings.

  4. 4

    Strength of schedule

    The aggregate difficulty of each tied team's full-season schedule, based on opponents' win percentages.

  5. 5

    Team Rating Score

    The conference's own computer rating system, combining results and schedule strength into a single score.

    Not simulated: SportSource Analytics' Team Rating Score isn't a feed this app ingests, so this step is skipped and the tiebreaker falls through to the next one.

  6. 6

    Random draw

    If every step above still leaves teams tied, the tie is broken by a random draw - in practice, a coin toss.

Official source: ACC Football Tiebreaker Policy

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the ACC Championship Game tiebreaker work?

When two or more teams finish tied atop the ACC standings, the conference's own published tiebreaker procedure decides the order - working through each of these 6 steps in turn until one of them produces a winner: Head-to-head record, then Combined record against common conference opponents, then Record against the next-highest-placed common opponent, working down the standings, then Strength of schedule, then Team Rating Score, then Random draw.

Did the ACC tiebreaker procedure change for the 2025 season?

No - ACC's tiebreaker procedure for 2025 is unchanged from the prior season.

Does strength of schedule affect ACC tiebreakers?

Yes - strength of schedule is the fourth step in ACC's published tiebreaker procedure, only reached if the steps before it don't separate the tied teams.

Do ACC tiebreakers depend on CFP committee rankings?

No - ACC's published tiebreaker procedure doesn't reference CFP committee rankings at any step.

What happens if a ACC tie is still unbroken after every tiebreaker step?

ACC's procedure falls back to a random draw (in practice, a coin toss) if every step before it - Head-to-head record, then Combined record against common conference opponents, then Record against the next-highest-placed common opponent, working down the standings, then Strength of schedule, then Team Rating Score - still leaves the tied teams even.
